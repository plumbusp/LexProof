The problem

AI quotes the law. AI gets it wrong.

Complaints to public bodies are increasingly written with AI. Many cite legal provisions that are misnumbered, misquoted or simply invented, and each false citation costs an officer time to check by hand.

What LexProof does

Every law a complaint cites, checked against the official text.

We find each reference, fetch the real text from legislation.gov.uk and compare them word for word. No fetched proof, no verdict.

What in Europe matters to UK public bodies

How much each threat flood (mass threat emails, coordinated complaint campaigns) and each complaint flood (people sending long AI-written complaints and requests) reported in Europe matters to UK councils, schools, the NHS and ombudsmen.

  • Fetch. Exa searches the live web for these floods in each country's own language, reads every article and checks each quote on the real page.
  • Filter. An Exa Agent (AI) checks every report against the client, here UK public bodies. Fixed rules turn its findings into a level from 1 to 5.
  • Show. One map of Europe for the client. 5 · Act now means it is hitting UK services now; older than 90 days drops one level.

Demo: news saved up to 14 Nov 2026. In production it updates every day. Another client country sees different levels.
