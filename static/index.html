The problem

AI quotes the law. AI gets it wrong.

Complaints to public bodies are increasingly written with AI. Many cite legal provisions that are misnumbered, misquoted or simply invented, and each false citation costs an officer time to check by hand.

What LexProof does

Every law a complaint cites, checked against the official text.

Letter arrow_forward Laws it cites arrow_forward Official text arrow_forward One status each

We find each reference, fetch the official text (legislation.gov.uk in this UK demo; each country's own law database elsewhere) and check what the letter claims against it. Every verdict comes with its source.

  • MATCHThe law exists, and no conflicting deadline or number was found.
  • MISQUOTEDThe law exists but says something different, for example another deadline.
  • NOT AS CITEDNo such section. We show the nearest real rule.
  • POSSIBLE RULENo law named. We show the rule that most likely applies.
  • NOT FOUNDNo matching law exists or could be found.

    • Fetch. Exa searches the web for these reports in each country's own language, reads every article and checks each quote on the real page.
