Formal Complaint and Request for Review: Homeless application reference HOM/26/0412

To the Housing Options Team, Camden Council,

I am writing to formally lodge a complaint, in the strongest possible terms, about the handling of my homeless application. I applied on 2 October 2026, after my landlord ended my tenancy. Since then my two children and I have been sleeping in my sister's front room, and on 6 November I was told by phone that the council owes me no main housing duty. No written decision has arrived.

Under section 184 of the Housing Act 1996, the council must make the inquiries needed to decide what duty it owes me, because it has reason to believe I may be homeless. Section 188 of the Housing Act 1996 requires the council to secure interim accommodation for us while those inquiries are made, and with two children we clearly have a priority need. None was offered.

Section 202 of the Housing Act 1996 gives me three months from the decision to ask for a review, and I am asking for one now. I also note that section 204Z of the Housing Act 1996 requires the council to pay my removal and storage costs within 14 days of any review, and the Homelessness (Families) Act 2021 entitles every family to a self-contained flat within 48 hours.

The council must respond to my review request within 10 working days.

If this is not put right, I will take my complaint to the Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman. Under section 26B of the Local Government Act 1974, I understand I have only 28 days to do so, so please treat this as urgent.

Yours faithfully,
Joanne Mercer
