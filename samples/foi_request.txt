Dear Information Governance Officer,

I am formally writing to request complete copies of all procurement documentation regarding the municipal contract awarded to CleanFlow Services Ltd for the 2025 Riverside Waste Management framework.

Under the Freedom of Information Act 2000, I am entitled to be told whether the council holds the information and to have that communicated to me promptly.

Furthermore, given the imminent public scrutiny hearing, you must reply no later than the tenth working day following this transmission. Please treat this matter with the statutory urgency it commands.

The requested disclosure must include the final scoring matrices, internal anonymised evaluation spreadsheets, and written officer recommendations. As you are aware, section 99 requires full disclosure of all contracts involving public expenditures exceeding fifty thousand pounds.

In addition to statutory FOI obligations, I also assert my right to inspect all council contracts under LGA 1972 s.100Z which precludes withholding commercially sensitive supplier scoring.

For the avoidance of doubt, please note that the council cannot refuse because of cost where public health infrastructure is concerned.

Finally, if you refuse you must say which exemption you rely on under FOIA s.17(1) and state clearly why the public interest favours withholding.

Yours sincerely,
J. Hartley
Northbridge Taxpayers Coalition
