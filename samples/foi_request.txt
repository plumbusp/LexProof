Subject: Freedom of Information request – road resurfacing contracts

Dear Northgate Borough Council,

Under section 1 of the Freedom of Information Act 2000, I request copies of all contracts the council signed for road resurfacing between January 2024 and September 2026, including the amounts paid to each contractor.

Under section 10 of the Act you must respond within 10 working days. The council must also reply to me in writing, and you are not allowed to charge me anything for this request.

Section 17A of the Freedom of Information Act 2000 says that a council cannot refuse a request on cost grounds. I also note that the Public Contracts Transparency Act 2019 requires councils to publish every contract over £500 within 7 days.

If any of this is environmental information, regulation 5 of the Environmental Information Regulations 2004 applies as well.

Yours faithfully,
J. Okafor
